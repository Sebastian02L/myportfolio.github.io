About me

Sebastián Loges is a videogame developer specializing in programming and software architecture. Since 2025, he has been working at Scientific Videogames, where he is responsible for designing and implementing modular, scalable, and maintainable software architectures.

His work includes gameplay and systems programming, integrating external tools and services, optimizing performance, and maintaining projects throughout their lifecycle. He has developed and deployed projects for a wide range of platforms, including Android, iOS, WebGL, VR, and AR, adapting both the software and its architecture to the technical requirements of each platform.

He primarily works with Unity and C#, with experience integrating technologies and services such as PlayFab and other third-party tools. He is particularly interested in solving technical challenges, building robust systems, and creating software that is efficient, scalable, and easy to maintain
